Check-In Crew Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Check-In Crew is a Reddit app that helps communities schedule recurring discussion posts and monthly accountability threads. This policy describes the data handled by the app in each subreddit where it is installed.

Data the app stores

The app stores its configuration in Reddit’s Devvit-managed storage for the relevant installation. This can include series names and schedules; post templates and future post edits; host usernames; maintainer Reddit account IDs and usernames; access-expiry dates; publication records containing post IDs, URLs, timestamps and the account that initiated a manual publication; and concise configuration and recovery records.

When a person uses the optional flair tool, the app reads their existing public Reddit flair and sends the selected flair text to Reddit to apply it. It does not retain the submitted flair fields. The optional calorie and BMI calculator runs in the browser; its inputs are not sent to or stored by Check-In Crew.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide scheduling, posting, host credit, maintainer access, publication history, recovery and the optional flair tool. Check-In Crew has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or external service integration. It does not collect participants’ goals, weights, comment contents, private messages, email addresses or passwords.

Retention and deletion

Series data remains until a moderator archives or permanently deletes the series. Archiving is reversible and retains data. Permanent series deletion removes its stored configuration, edits and publication history; deleting a Reddit post does not delete comments underneath it.

For a verified legal privacy request, such as a GDPR erasure request, an Everything-permissions moderator can use Check-In Crew’s Personal-data deletion tool. It removes the requested username from stored access, host, audit, recovery and publication-actor records, and redacts matching credits in recorded posts created by the app. The tool shows its progress and supports retrying failed Reddit post edits. It does not search for or change unrelated Reddit content, comments, flair or Reddit account data.

Your choices and contact

Do not include personal information in a flair unless you choose to make it public beside your Reddit username. To request access, correction or deletion of data handled by a Check-In Crew installation for r/loseit, contact the moderation team through r/loseit modmail. For another community, contact that community’s moderators.
