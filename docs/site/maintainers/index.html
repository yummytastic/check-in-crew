Check-In Crew

Maintainer quickstart

This guide is for moderators and volunteer maintainers who have been assigned access to a Check-In Crew series.

Open the dashboard

  1. Open the subreddit menu and choose Open Check-In Crew dashboard.
  2. The first time, confirm that Check-In Crew may create its locked dashboard post.
  3. Choose Open dashboard and select the series you manage.

If you cannot open a series, ask a moderator to assign your Reddit account as a maintainer.

Review upcoming threads

  1. Choose the month to review monthly posts, or review the upcoming window for a recurring series.
  2. Open a post to view its title, body, date, time and timezone.
  3. Use Edit to change an upcoming post, then preview it and apply the change.
  4. Changes affect that upcoming post only unless you change the series defaults in the dashboard.

Pause or resume a series

Select the series and choose Pause selected when posting should stop temporarily. Resume it from the dashboard after checking the next dates and content. Pausing does not delete saved posts or history.

Hosts and credits

Hosts receive credit in generated posts. Host credit and management access are separate, so a person can be a host, a maintainer, both or neither. Ask a moderator to change the People & access list if someone needs a role added or removed.

When something goes wrong

  • If a post exists on Reddit but the dashboard shows it as pending, use Fix a missing publication record from the subreddit menu.
  • If a scheduled post is not ready, check the series timezone, pause state and next scheduled date before retrying.
  • For access, deletion or policy questions, contact the community moderators through modmail.
